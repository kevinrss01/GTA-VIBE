# Weapon assets

Every weapon the player can hold is a generated Tripo model. Two of the four
were generated by the gun-shop workstream for its display case and are reused
here unchanged, so a weapon looks the same on the rack as it does in the
player's hands; the other two were generated for the combat layer.

All four arrive normalised to 1.0 on their longest axis with a CENTRE pivot and
their own node scaling. `src/combat/WeaponViewmodel.ts` measures each one after
loading, rescales it to the length below and re-origins it on its own centre,
exactly as `src/world/ModelLibrary.ts` does for the street furniture - the
transform in the file is never trusted.

THE LENGTHS BELOW ARE FRAMING, NOT PHYSICS. A real 1.12 m carbine held 40 cm
from a 62-degree eye fills half the screen and puts its butt inside the near
plane. The gun shop draws the same files at their real world size on its rack,
where the viewer is metres away and the real size is what reads correctly.

## A known generation artefact

The generator read "blued steel" as the colour blue and returned a cobalt
shotgun. `defaultViewmodels()` carries a `tint` for that one weapon which
halves the blue channel of its base colour map, turning it back into gunmetal;
it is one material colour, not a shader, and it costs nothing at runtime. A
regeneration with a plainer prompt - "dark grey steel", no colour words that
name a colour - would be the proper fix.

## Prompts

Both new prompts follow the house style the gun shop established: an original
design, described as a prop, single fused mesh, neutral studio lighting, no
stand and no text.

- `smg` — "game-ready compact submachine gun prop, side profile, short barrel
  with folding wire stock, matte black polymer receiver and dark steel bolt,
  straight box magazine, clean readable silhouette, single fused mesh, PBR
  materials, neutral studio lighting, no stand, no base, no text"
- `shotgun` — "game-ready pump-action shotgun prop, side profile, short blued
  steel barrel and receiver, ribbed dark wood fore-end and stock, clean
  readable silhouette, single fused mesh, PBR materials, neutral studio
  lighting, no stand, no base, no text"

Parameters for both: `model_version=P1-20260311`, `face_limit=1400`,
`texture_alignment=geometry`, PBR and texture on. Source artifacts, previews
and `task.json` are under `tripo-out/weapons/`.

## Orientation

Three of the four are modelled nose-along-Z and the carbine nose-along-X. The
per-weapon `yaw` in `defaultViewmodels()` is what reconciles them; the assets
themselves were not re-exported.

## Validation

`node tools/inspect-glb.mjs <file>` was run on all four. Each is a single mesh
with one material and three textures, no animations and no skins, and reports
`transforms ok`. Triangle counts are in the table above: the whole armoury is
5 798 triangles, of which at most 2 020 are ever drawn at once.
